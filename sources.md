## Source discipline

- Audited historical numbers come from SEC filings.
- Current quarterly figures and guidance come from NVIDIA and are labeled accordingly.
- Management forecasts and opinions are not treated as audited facts.
- Reuters is used to add independent context, not to replace primary filings.
- The frozen Edition A remains unchanged; corrections and expansions appear only in the dated report.
