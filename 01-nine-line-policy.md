1. Decision user: Me, acting as a student equity analyst selecting one company for Project 1.
Decision: Choose one publicly traded operating company for an FCFF-based valuation.
Universe: U.S.-listed, non-financial operating companies with at least five years of public financial statements.
Exclusions: Banks, insurers, REITs, funds, shells, pre-revenue firms, and other non-operating vehicles.
Two criteria: Durable operating profitability and consistent conversion of operating earnings into free cash flow.
Definitions: Durable profitability means positive EBIT in each of the last five fiscal years; consistent cash conversion means positive FCFF in at least four of those five years.
Thresholds/weights: Require both minimums, then rank qualifying companies 60% on five-year average ROIC and 40% on five-year FCFF stability.
Success test: The selected company has sufficient operating history and disclosures to support a defensible enterprise-value forecast without relying primarily on speculative growth.
Overriding disqualifier: Exclude even the top-ranked company if its latest filings reveal that one customer accounts for more than 30% of revenue.
